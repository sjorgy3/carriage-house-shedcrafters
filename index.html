FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call or text 801-580-7925.

How much does a custom sauna cost?

It depends on size, indoor vs. outdoor, the wood and the heater. After a free on-site visit we send a written, itemized quote so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How much space do I need for a golf simulator?

A good rule of thumb is about 10 ft of ceiling height, 12–15 ft of width and 15–18 ft of depth. Smaller rooms can often work too. We'll measure and check your full swing during the visit.

How long does a build take?

Most indoor saunas and simulator rooms take one to three weeks once materials arrive. Outdoor builds depend on the foundation and the weather.

Do you handle electrical and permits?

Yes. We coordinate the electrical work and any permits your project needs, so you don't have to manage separate contractors.

Can you build a sauna inside a backyard shed?

Yes. Backyard buildings are what we've built for years, so an outdoor sauna house, or a shed with a sauna inside, is right in our wheelhouse.

What areas do you serve?

We build on-site along the Wasatch Front, from Park City to Grantsville.
