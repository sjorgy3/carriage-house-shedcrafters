FAQ

Common questions

How much does a custom sauna cost?

It depends on size, indoor vs. outdoor, the wood you choose and the heater. After a free consultation we give you an itemized quote, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How much space do I need for a golf simulator?

As a rough guide, about 10 ft of ceiling height, 12–15 ft of width and 15–18 ft of depth makes a comfortable room. Smaller spaces can often work too. We'll measure and check your full swing during the consult.

How long does a build take?

Most indoor saunas and simulator rooms take one to three weeks once materials arrive. Outdoor saunas can take longer depending on the foundation and weather.

Do you handle electrical and permits?

Yes. We coordinate the electrical work and any permits your project needs, so you don't have to manage separate contractors.

Can you build a sauna in a backyard shed or outbuilding?

Yes. Backyard outbuildings are what we've built for years, so an outdoor sauna house, or a shed with a sauna inside, is right in our wheelhouse. We can also pair a simulator room and a sauna in one project.

What areas do you serve?

We build on-site along the Wasatch Front, from Park City to Grantsville. Not sure if you're in range? Call or text 801-580-7925.
